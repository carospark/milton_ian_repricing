Trading coverage behind the study

Event interval: −4/+3 calendar days. Full inventory: −45/+60, or 106 dates. Several prints on one date do not form a trajectory. Identity eligibility is not verified tranche exposure. Counts overlap across storms and cannot be summed into a distinct market-wide bond total.

Finding · pre-landfall matches remain thin

The most recent strictly earlier named-market Yes-price sample is matched to bond execution. These are token-price observations, not executable quotes or proof of market liquidity.

Calendar days −4/−1 only; landfall-day trades are excluded. Trades are deduplicated across contracts. Ian has no usable named-storm series in the screen; Milton’s seasonal city thresholds are proxies, not named-Milton probabilities.

Storm roles and usable sample

Weather, markets and bond risk are different outcomes

GIS wind probabilities: all basins / all active storms; cumulative local threshold exceedance over 0–120 hours at 34, 50 and 64 knots. Two fixed half-degree locations per storm are context, not exposure weights.

Nine UTC dates, −7/+1, every six hours. Beryl July 9 06/12/18 UTC cycles are not listed in the cached index. Missing points and threshold layers remain missing. Cycle initialization is not publication time.

Prediction markets: Beryl’s two contracts measure US hurricane / tropical-storm landfall, not Texas loss severity. Idalia’s two contracts measure Florida landfall / intensity. Milton has no verified named-storm contract in the local archive; seasonal city thresholds remain proxies. Archive absence does not establish that no market ever existed.

Eastern execution clocks follow the source cleaner’s execution-date/time mapping and FINRA documentation; daylight-saving rules are applied before UTC conversion. Invalid and ambiguous clocks remain unmatched. No NHC first-publication-time match is asserted.

Finding · loss labels need a prose check

Ian: 13 source rows; one total-loss label conflicts with provisional mark-to-market wording. Two single-storm terminal-loss prose candidates remain, but neither currently has an exact source-class link to the strict eligible bond sample.

Milton: three source rows, including two multi-event rows; no terminal-loss candidate passes the current screen. Idalia / Beryl: no matching rows in this source, not proof of no losses.

Do not propagate deal-level outcomes to every tranche. Source event dates are not verified loss-announcement timestamps; a terminal-loss prose candidate is not a confirmed loss on a linked bond.

Source review is partial

Expanded review candidates have trading on both sides of landfall or named-market overlap. This is a different sample from the original 23 Beryl same-date-overlap bonds.

Secondary-source geography is kept separate from offering-document verification. Amendments, active risk periods, remaining coverage, exact classes and industry-index weights need evidence before a loss attribution or target-state portfolio is claimed.

Pending assets remain explicitly pending. Source repositories are read-only; conflicts are recorded in the study rather than silently rewritten upstream.
